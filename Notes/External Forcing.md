> External forcing refers to a forcing factor outside the [[climate]] system that causes a change in the climate system - [[IPCC AR4]] | [[IPCC_DA_Good_Practice_Guidance_Hegerl_et_al_2010.pdf#page=3&annotation=109R|Paper]] 

In the [[IPCC WG2]] community, forcing often refers to a wider set of influences in impact studies that are external to the system under study and that may or may not include climate.
