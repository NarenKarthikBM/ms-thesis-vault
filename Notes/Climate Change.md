> A change in the state of the *[[Climate|climate]]* that can be identified (e.g., by using statistical tests) by changes in the mean and/or the variability of its properties and that persists for an extended period, typically decades or longer. - [[IPCC]]

> A change of climate which is attributed directly or indirectly to human activity that alters the composition of the global atmosphere and which is in addition to natural climate variability observed over comparable time periods - [[UNFCC]]

Note that UNFCC considers that climate change is attributed to human activity and [[Climate Variability|climate variability]] is attributed to natural causes.
