- These represent the assessment reports about knowledge on [[Climate Change|climate change]], its causes, potential impacts and response options, approved by the IPCC Working groups ([[IPCC WG1]], [[IPCC WG2]] and [[IPCC WG3]])
![[Pasted image 20261013175011.png]]

- The list of Assessment reports are as follows
	1. [[IPCC FAR| 1st Assessment Report]] (1990) : underlined global importance and consequences of climate change and encouraged international cooperation
	2. [[IPCC SAR|2nd Assessment report]] (1995): provided resources for governments in the run up to [[Kyoto Protocol]] (1997)
	3. [[IPCC TAR| 3rd Assessment Report]] (2001) : focused attention on impacts of climate change and need for mitigation
	4. [[IPCC AR4| 4th Assessment Report]] (2007) : laid the groundwork for post-[[Kyoto Protocol]] agreement focusing on limiting warming to 2$^{\circ}$ C.
	5. [[IPCC AR5|5th Assessment Report]] (2013-14) : provided scientific input for the [[Paris Agreement]]
	6. [[IPCC AR6|6th Assessment Report]] (2023) : the latest and the most detailed report cycle containing a synthesis report, three [[IPCC Special Reports|special reports]] and a refinement of the [[IPCC Methodology Report|methodology report]]
	7. [[IPCC AR7|7th Assessment report]] (ongoing) : the upcoming report expected to complete by late 2029
